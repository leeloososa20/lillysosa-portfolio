Overview

  • Product: The Inspiration Tab in YouTube Studio
  • My role: UX Lead
  • Team: 1 UX Lead (me), 1 Visual Designer, 1 Interaction Designer, 1 Content Designer, 1 UX Researcher, 2 PMs, 18+ Engineers
  • Timeline: 6 months from idea to launch
  • Core challenge: Pivot a research-focused product ('Trends') into a generative AI tool to meet the rapidly evolving needs of creators, navigating extreme ambiguity and a highly aggressive timeline.

My role & approach

As UX Lead, I drove the strategic vision and roadmap for this Gen AI experience. I fostered a highly collaborative environment, working with visual design, interaction design, content design, and research to deeply understand and iterate with creators. This agile approach allowed us to move swiftly from initial concept to production in a rapid three-month cycle, laying critical foundations for a scalable product.

The challenge

As YouTube creators rapidly adopted GenAI tools in early 2024, we pivoted from the research-focused 'Trends' tab to develop the 'Inspiration' tab, a generative tool designed to support creators' evolving needs.

Impact

This Gen AI experience empowers millions of creators to unlock their full creative potential by facilitating brainstorming. It marked a pivotal shift in how YouTube supports evolving creator needs.
